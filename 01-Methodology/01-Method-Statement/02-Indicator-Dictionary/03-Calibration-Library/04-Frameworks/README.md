# Frameworks

Status: Active Development

## Purpose

This folder contains all supporting frameworks that power the RGA NeuroMatrix™ Incident Management System.

These frameworks support:

- Support Workers
- Team Leaders
- Managers
- Trainers
- Auditors
- AI Systems

---

## Framework Library

### RGA TRACE™

Incident report writing framework.

Components:

- Timeline
- Response
- Accurate Observations
- Communication & Dignity
- Escalation

Purpose:

Provide support workers with a simple and repeatable process for writing high-quality incident reports.

Status: Approved

---

### RGA S.A.F.E.R.™

First-response framework.

Components:

- Secure Safety
- Assist & Care
- Flag To Supervisor
- Evidence Preserved
- Record

Purpose:

Guide workers through the first minutes following an incident.

Status: Approved

---

### Three-Lens Model™

Quality assurance model.

Lens 1:
Writer

Lens 2:
Reviewer

Lens 3:
Manager

Purpose:

Establish quality controls throughout the incident lifecycle.

Status: Approved

---

### Camera Test™

Observation framework.

Question:

Could a camera have recorded this?

Purpose:

Reduce subjective language and increase objective documentation.

Status: Approved

---

### Observation Ladder™

Evidence framework.

Level 1:
Observed

Level 2:
Reported By Others

Level 3:
Interpretation

Purpose:

Help workers distinguish observation from interpretation.

Status: Approved

---

### Read-Aloud Test™

Quality check framework.

Question:

Would I be comfortable reading this report aloud to:

- Participant
- Family Member
- Manager
- Auditor

Purpose:

Improve professionalism and participant dignity.

Status: Approved

---

### Tell Now / Tell Today / Record™

Escalation framework.

Purpose:

Help workers make immediate escalation decisions.

Status: Approved

---

### Awareness-Time Ledger™

Time tracking framework.

Captures:

- Incident Occurred
- Worker Aware
- Team Leader Aware
- Manager Aware

Purpose:

Support incident escalation and management accountability.

Status: Approved
