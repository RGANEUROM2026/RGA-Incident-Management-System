# Indicator Dictionary

Status: Complete

## Purpose

The Indicator Dictionary defines all assessment indicators used within the RGA™ Incident Report Quality Matrix.

It provides the detailed rules, examples, coaching guidance and scoring logic required to ensure consistent assessment of incident reports.

---

## Matrix Dimensions

### D1 – Safety & Response

Assessment of immediate actions taken following an incident.

### D2 – Timeline & Completeness

Assessment of chronology, timing and completeness of information.

### D3 – Objectivity

Assessment of factual accuracy and removal of subjective language.

### D4 – Participant Voice & Dignity

Assessment of participant-centred documentation and respectful language.

### D5 – Escalation

Assessment of notification and escalation requirements.

### D6 – Evidence & Context

Assessment of evidence, observations and contextual information.

### D7 – Privacy & Professional Standards

Assessment of confidentiality and professional documentation standards.

### D8 – Follow-Up & Risk

Assessment of ongoing actions, monitoring and risk management.
